# LONGSHOT_PATCHES.md - every change to Godot's core in this fork (docs/GODOT_REBUILD_PROGRAM.md section 2)

One row per patch. No patch without the number that justified it. Each is a separate `[patch]` commit on
`longshot-4.7` so the branch rebases onto upstream releases; anything general is sent upstream.

| # | Where | What | The number | Upstream |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `scene/3d/node_3d.cpp` `Node3D::look_at_from_position` | the "same position" guard compares with an ABSOLUTE epsilon (`distance_squared_to < CMP_EPSILON^2`) instead of `Vector3::is_equal_approx`, whose tolerance is 1e-5 of the coordinate | the precision proof (2026-09-21): at the pad, 6,371,839 m from the Earth-centred origin, `look_at` refused a target 12 m away (tolerance 64 m); on the Moon, 384,400 km away, the tolerance would be 3.8 km | to send: a true-scale fix any large-world project needs |
| 2 | `scene/resources/compositor.h/.cpp` `CompositorEffect` | a native virtual `render_callback_native(int, const RenderData *)` called before the script virtual in `_call_render_callback` | `_render_callback` is a GDVIRTUAL (script / extension only): a C++ module cannot implement a compositor effect without it; the clouds module (day 7, 2026-09-22) is the first user | to send: engine modules with compositor effects need it |
| 3 | `servers/rendering/renderer_rd/forward_clustered/render_forward_clustered.cpp` `_fill_render_list` | an instance whose surfaces all draw in the transparent pass has `INSTANCE_DATA_FLAG_USE_GI_BUFFERS` cleared: the GI buffers (the half-res ambient and reflection from the opaque pass's depth and normal-roughness) describe the opaque surface at the pixel, and a transparent surface without forward GI (`gi_mode` disabled) was reading them and taking the GI of whatever stood behind it | the sea (2026-09-22, the ring-step slice of docs/manual/ocean.md): a transparent water surface with GI disabled in an SDFGI scene showed a warm patch where the seabed's bounce reflection came through the buffers - its specular term alone read (0.178, 0.216, 0.276) in the patch against (0.153, 0.214, 0.287) beside it with SDFGI on, and a uniform (0.12, 0.20, 0.30) with SDFGI off; the sky's radiance is the only right reflection for it | to send: any transparent material with GI disabled in an SDFGI / VoxelGI scene is lit by its background's GI |
| 4 | `servers/rendering/rendering_server_enums.h` (`INSTANCE_FLAG_IGNORE_GI`), `renderer_geometry_instance.h/.cpp`, `renderer_scene_cull.h/.cpp`, `renderer_rd/forward_clustered/render_forward_clustered.cpp`, `doc/classes/RenderingServer.xml` | a server instance flag: the instance neither contributes to nor receives global illumination - no SDFGI (forward or buffered), no VoxelGI - whatever its `gi_mode`; its indirect light is the environment's ambient and the reflected light (the sky's radiance, the reflection probes, SSR) | the sea in the opaque pass (2026-09-22, the SSR slice of docs/manual/ocean.md): with `gi_mode` disabled an opaque surface still reads the GI buffers (Godot's documented behaviour), and for a roughness-0.06 water gi.glsl replaces 30% of the reflection with its SDF trace inside the cascades (`reflection_light.a = alpha * (1 - 5 x roughness) + 5 x roughness`), so the sea inside 819 m read 0.02 brighter than beyond with a faint edge at the last cascade (the smoke's GI court: SDFGI on / off 0.0195 apart, the straight-step 0.0131); a water surface is a mirror of the sky and the screen, never of the probes | to send: the proposal for a per-instance "receive GI" switch (water, sky domes, holograms) |
